## Description

GEOG 172 is designed to help students learn quantitative methods for the analysis of geographical data. Topics include spatial clustering, spatial auto-correlation, spatial regression, and introductory methods for analyzing point, area (lattice), and continuous data. Tutorial includes the use of statistical software for exploratory spatial data analysis. The R software environment will be used for statistical computing and graphics throughout.

[Schedule](pages/schedule.md) of lectures is roughly outlined here, but may be adjusted as the course progresses.

## Tutorials

[Tutorials](pages/tutorials.md) sessions will be held weekly to provide hands-on experience with the concepts and methods covered in the lectures. Students will work in small groups to solve problems, but will need to complete seven tutorial assignments individually.

## Readings

[Readings](pages/readings.md) will be primarily drawn from Rogerson (2021) *Spatial Statistical Methods for Geography* and O'Sullivan and Unwin (2010) *Geographic Information Analysis*, and are listed alongside the schedule. Students are expected to read the assigned materials before each class. Electronic version of both texts are available through the UCSB library, and will be provided on Canvas. In selected weeks readings for the course will come from other texts. Additional readings are provided to allow students to explore topics they find interesting, though these are not required.

## Resources

[Resources](pages/resources.md) designed to facilitate student learning include freely available, online resources developed by scholars independent of this course, and materials developed specifically for this course. Most lectures will come with code demos that will contain examples of problems very much like the ones that students will be asked to do in their tutorial assignments.
