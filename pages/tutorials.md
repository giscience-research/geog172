## Overview
Students will have the opportunity to complete seven tutorial assignments each worth 10 percent of their overall grade.
Students will receive credit for the best six of those seven assignments, which will account for up to 60 percent of their overall grade.
Tutorial assignments will be due at 5pm one week after the tutorial session in which they were assigned (e.g., 5pm the following Thursday or Friday)
